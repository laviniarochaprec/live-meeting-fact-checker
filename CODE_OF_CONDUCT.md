# Code of Conduct

We want this to be a place where people can disagree about ideas without it getting personal.

**Do**
- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give specific, constructive feedback.
- Welcome newcomers. Everyone here is learning something.
- Credit other people's ideas.

**Do not**
- Harass, insult or belittle anyone.
- Share other people's private information, or confidential material from your employer.
- Post spam or unrelated promotion.

**Reporting.** If something is not okay, open a private security advisory on the repository or contact the maintainer through the profile linked on the repository page. Reports are handled with discretion.

**Enforcement.** The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close threads, or block accounts that break these rules.

This is a short adaptation of widely used community standards. English and Portuguese are both welcome here.
