Live Meeting Fact-Checker BETA

Catches wrong numbers as they are said, and answers quick questions, using your metric dictionary.

Made to adapt to any company: each one describes its metrics in a dictionary and points them at its own database. Connectors to real databases are not built yet; in this beta the dictionary holds the (fictional) numbers itself. How it will connect.

All sample data is fictional ("Acme Cloud" does not exist). Everything runs in your browser: your dictionary, sentences and audio are never uploaded. The only download is the speech model (40 to 80 MB), once, from a public CDN, if you use the Live tab.

Type a sentence

Try: Churn in March was 6% · Active customers in June were 12,900 · What does MRR mean? · NPS in March was 60

Or paste a whole meeting

One line per sentence group, as Name: what they said.

Listen to a real conversation

The speech-to-text model runs in your browser. Audio never leaves your computer. Speech is matched in English for now.

Meeting tab: in Chrome or Edge, pick the browser tab with your call and tick "Share tab audio". Without headphones, the microphone also hears the speakers; the app ignores the microphone while the tab is speaking. You can use both at once.
